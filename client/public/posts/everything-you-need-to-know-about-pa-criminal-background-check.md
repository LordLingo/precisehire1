### **Introduction to PA Criminal Background Check Clearance**

A Pennsylvania \(PA\) criminal background check clearance is a document that certifies an individual’s criminal history, or lack thereof, within the state of Pennsylvania. This clearance is an essential requirement for various purposes, including employment, volunteer work, and other legal obligations.

The clearance is particularly crucial for individuals working in positions where they are responsible for the safety and well-being of vulnerable populations, such as children, the elderly, or individuals with disabilities. These may include teachers, healthcare professionals, and government employees, among others.

For many employers and organizations, a criminal background check clearance is a mandatory part of the hiring process. It helps ensure that employees and volunteers are trustworthy, reliable, and have no criminal history that could pose a risk in certain work environments.

### **How to Obtain a PA Criminal Background Check Clearance**

Obtaining a PA criminal background check clearance involves several steps. Below, we will explain the process from start to finish.

* * *

### **Step 1: Submit a Request**

The first step in obtaining a PA criminal background check clearance is submitting a request to the Pennsylvania State Police or the relevant authorized agency. This can be done online or in person, depending on the method of application chosen.

  * **Online Request** : The Pennsylvania Access to Criminal History \(PATCH\) system allows individuals to request a criminal background check online through the official Pennsylvania State Police website.
  * **In-Person Request** : Applicants can visit an authorized fingerprinting location or law enforcement agency to complete the necessary paperwork.

* * *

### **Step 2: Provide Required Information**

Applicants will need to provide specific information to process the background check, such as:

  * Full name
  * Date of birth
  * Social Security number
  * Fingerprints \(if applicable\)

In some cases, additional documentation may be required, such as proof of identity or a reason for the background check \(e.g., employment or volunteer work\).

* * *

### **Step 3: Receiving the Clearance**

After the request is processed, the applicant will receive their criminal background check clearance. This clearance document confirms whether or not there is a criminal record associated with the applicant. It is then provided to the organization or employer requiring the clearance for review.

* * *

### **Step 4: Review by the Employer or Organization**

Once the clearance document is received, the employer or organization will review the details of the background check. If there is a criminal record, they will assess the nature of the offense and determine whether it disqualifies the applicant from the position.

* * *

### **Step 5: Final Decision**

After the employer or organization reviews the clearance, they will make a final decision based on the applicant’s criminal history \(if any\). If the clearance is clear, the applicant can move forward with the hiring or volunteering process. If there is a criminal record, the organization may choose to reject the applicant, depending on their policies and the nature of the offense.

There are several ways to obtain a PA criminal background check clearance, with each method varying in terms of time, cost, and convenience. Here is a breakdown of the different options:

#### **Online Application via the Pennsylvania Access to Criminal History \(PATCH\) System**

The most common and convenient way to apply for a PA criminal background check is through the Pennsylvania State Police’s online system, the PATCH system. This allows individuals to request their criminal history report directly from the comfort of their home.

##### **Process** :

  1. **Visit the PATCH website** : Start by navigating to the official Pennsylvania State Police’s PATCH system website.
  2. **Create an Account** : You will need to create an account if you are a first-time user.
  3. **Fill Out the Request Form** : Provide the necessary information, including your full name, date of birth, and social security number.
  4. **Pay the Fee** : The cost for an online request is typically around $22, which is paid directly through the website.
  5. **Receive the Clearance** : After processing \(usually within 24-48 hours\), you will receive your clearance via email.

##### **Advantages** :

  * Quick and easy process
  * Available 24/7
  * Immediate results

##### **Disadvantages** :

  * Requires internet access and a credit card for payment

* * *

#### **In-Person Request at Authorized Locations**

For individuals who prefer not to use the online system, or for those without internet access, an in-person request is an option. This can be done at local law enforcement agencies or other authorized fingerprinting centers.

##### **Process** :

  1. **Locate an Authorized Location** : Find a local law enforcement agency or a designated fingerprinting location.
  2. **Provide Identification and Details** : Be prepared to provide proof of identification, such as a driver’s license, along with your full name and date of birth.
  3. **Pay the Fee** : The fee for an in-person request is typically around $22, paid at the location.
  4. **Get Fingerprinted** : Depending on the request, you may be required to submit fingerprints.
  5. **Wait for Processing** : The processing time for in-person requests may take a few days, depending on the location.

##### **Advantages** :

  * Personal assistance if needed
  * Can submit fingerprints in person

##### **Disadvantages** :

  * Requires travel to an authorized location
  * Longer processing times

### **FAQs About PA Criminal Background Check Clearance**

###  __How long does it take to get a PA criminal background check clearance?

The processing time for a PA criminal background check clearance typically takes 24 to 48 hours if submitted through the Pennsylvania State Police’s PATCH system. However, in-person requests or third-party services may take longer, depending on the method and location.

###  __What records are included in a PA criminal background check?

A PA criminal background check includes information about an individual’s criminal history within the state, such as arrests, convictions, and any pending charges. It does not include records from other states or federal agencies, unless specifically requested.

###  __Can employers require PA criminal background check clearance for all positions?

While employers can request a criminal background check for most positions, it is especially required for roles that involve working with children, vulnerable adults, or sensitive information. Pennsylvania law mandates that certain professionals, including healthcare workers, teachers, and law enforcement officers, undergo criminal background checks as a condition of employment.

###  __Can a criminal record prevent me from getting a job in Pennsylvania?

A criminal record may influence hiring decisions, particularly for jobs in sensitive sectors, such as healthcare, education, or finance. However, each employer has their own policies regarding criminal history, and some may provide opportunities for individuals with a criminal background based on the nature of the offense and how long ago it occurred.

###  __What happens if there is a mistake on my criminal background check?

If you believe there is a mistake on your criminal background check, you can dispute the information. You should contact the Pennsylvania State Police or the agency responsible for processing the background check. They will guide you through the process of rectifying any errors or inaccuracies.

###  __How do I ensure that my PA criminal background check is valid?

To ensure the validity of your PA criminal background check, make sure that you obtain the clearance from an official source, such as the Pennsylvania State Police or a reputable third-party service like **PreciseHire**. Double-check the information on your clearance report, and confirm that all details are accurate before submitting it to an employer or organization.
