## **What is an FBI Background Check?**

An **FBI Background Check** is a comprehensive screening process conducted by the Federal Bureau of Investigation \(FBI\) to assess an individual’s criminal history. It is widely used in various sectors for security purposes, including employment screening, immigration applications, gun purchases, adoption processes, and more. The FBI background check primarily focuses on national criminal databases, checking for any arrests, convictions, and other criminal-related information tied to an individual’s identity, such as fingerprints or personal identifiers.

The FBI’s National Crime Information Center \(NCIC\) is the primary source for this check, containing extensive information about criminal records across the country. The results of an FBI background check can be crucial for making informed decisions, especially when it comes to employment or legal matters. For example, employers and immigration authorities use this background check to ensure an applicant’s background aligns with their eligibility or standards.

The importance of an FBI [background check](<https://www.mycriminalrecords.com/>) cannot be overstated, as it helps organizations, government agencies, and individuals make safe, informed decisions. Here are a few reasons why it is commonly requested:

  * **Employment Screening** : Employers, particularly in industries like healthcare, finance, and education, often require FBI background checks to ensure that candidates have no criminal history that could affect the workplace.
  * **Immigration and Visa Applications** : When applying for visas or U.S. citizenship, individuals must submit an FBI background check to prove they have no criminal record that would disqualify them from entering or staying in the country.
  * **Adoption** : Prospective adoptive parents are often required to undergo an FBI background check as part of the adoption process to ensure the safety of the child.
  * **Gun Purchases** : Federal laws require individuals purchasing firearms to undergo a background check that includes FBI records to ensure that the buyer is legally eligible to own a weapon.
  * **Security Clearance** : Individuals applying for security clearances with government agencies undergo FBI background checks to evaluate whether they are fit to handle classified information.

### **Methods of Obtaining an FBI Background Check**

When it comes to obtaining an FBI [background check](<https://precisehire.com/the-timeline-of-fingerprint-background-checks-explained/>), there are several methods available, ranging from traditional in-person procedures to faster, online options. These methods can vary in terms of cost, convenience, and processing time.

  1. **Traditional Methods \(Mail Requests\)** : 
     * **Direct Mail Request** : Traditionally, individuals would need to fill out a fingerprint card and submit it by mail to the FBI, along with a request form and payment. This process can take weeks or even months to complete, as it involves processing time at both the local and federal levels.
  2. **Online Requests** : 
     * **FBI’s Identity History Summary Checks** : The FBI offers an online service where individuals can submit their fingerprints and a request for a background check. While this method can be faster than mailing documents, it still requires time for fingerprinting and submitting the necessary information.
  3. **Third-Party Services** : 
     * **Authorized Third-Party Providers** : There are several private companies and services that act as intermediaries between the applicant and the FBI. These providers can expedite the process by handling the fingerprinting, submitting the request, and providing faster results. Some of these services offer FBI background checks within 24-48 hours, depending on the level of service requested.

**Step-by-Step Process for Requesting an FBI Background Check**

![Step-by-Step Process for Requesting an FBI Background Check](https://precisehire.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Step-by-Step-Process-for-Requesting-an-FBI-Background-Check-visual-selection-png.webp)

Whether you choose to submit a request directly through the FBI or use a third-party provider, the process remains relatively straightforward. Here’s an overview of the steps involved in obtaining an FBI background check quickly:

  1. **Complete the Request Form** : 
     * To begin, you must fill out the **Identity History Summary Request Form** \(form I-783\) on the FBI’s website. The form requires basic personal information, such as your name, address, and date of birth. You will also need to specify whether you want a **self-request** or if the report will be used for employment, immigration, or other purposes.
  2. **Fingerprinting** : 
     * Fingerprints are essential for the background check process. You can either submit your fingerprints electronically through a **Live Scan provider** or use traditional **inked fingerprint cards**. For faster processing, Live Scan is the most efficient method.
  3. **Submit the Request** : 
     * Once your fingerprinting is complete, submit your fingerprints and form. If using a third-party service, the company may handle the submission process for you. If submitting directly to the FBI, you will need to mail in your fingerprints along with the required payment.
  4. **Wait for Processing** : 
     * The FBI will process your request and generate a report based on the criminal history database. Processing times can vary, but online requests and Live Scan typically take less time than traditional mail submissions.
  5. **Receive Your Report** : 
     * Once processed, the FBI will send the report to you or your authorized recipient. If using a third-party provider, they may deliver the report directly to you, often electronically for even faster access.

**Legal Aspects of FBI Background Checks**

![Legal Aspects of FBI Background Checks](https://precisehire.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Legal-Aspects-of-FBI-Background-Checks-visual-selection-png.webp)

It’s important to remember that FBI background checks are subject to several legal regulations, ensuring that individuals’ privacy rights are respected, and that the process remains fair and consistent. Key legal considerations include:

  1. **FCRA Compliance \(Fair Credit Reporting Act\)** : The FCRA is a federal law that governs how background check information can be collected, used, and shared. It ensures that individuals are informed when a background check is being conducted, and that the information is accurate, relevant, and not misleading.
  2. **EEOC Guidelines \(Equal Employment Opportunity Commission\)** : For employment purposes, the **EEOC** prohibits discrimination based on race, color, national origin, sex, disability, or religion when using background checks. Employers must ensure that background checks are used fairly and consistently without bias.
  3. **State Laws** : Each state may have additional laws and regulations governing background checks, including restrictions on the types of criminal records that can be considered for employment or housing. Be sure to check with local authorities regarding the regulations in your area.
