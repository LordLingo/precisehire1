Through this verification, employers and licensing agencies can confirm that individuals meet the necessary criteria for specific roles or responsibilities. Given the increasing concerns about security and integrity, fingerprint [background checks](<https://www.mycriminalrecords.com/>) have become indispensable in Tennessee’s workforce, especially in sectors like healthcare, education, and government.

#### What is a Fingerprint Background Check?

A **fingerprint background check** is a process where an individual’s fingerprints are collected and used to search various national and state criminal databases for any prior criminal history. This method is more accurate than traditional name-based background checks because it uses biometric identifiers—namely fingerprints—that are unique to each person. As a result, fingerprint-based checks reduce the likelihood of errors or mismatches that can occur with name or date-of-birth-based searches.

In Tennessee, fingerprint background checks are frequently required for people who want to work in sensitive positions or receive certain types of licenses. These checks offer a level of assurance that individuals do not have disqualifying criminal convictions that could impact their fitness for specific roles.

For example, fingerprint checks are a common requirement for those applying for jobs in high-security environments, such as government positions or roles involving access to sensitive data. Similarly, healthcare professionals, teachers, and childcare workers are subject to fingerprint background checks to ensure they are not a risk to the people they serve.

Obtaining a **TN fingerprint background check** involves a systematic process that includes several steps, from fingerprint collection to processing and reporting the results. #### Step-by-Step Process for Obtaining a TN Fingerprint Background Check

The process for obtaining a fingerprint background check in Tennessee can be broken down into several key steps:

  1. **Prepare Required Documents** Before visiting a fingerprinting location, ensure that you have all necessary documents. Commonly required documents include: 
     * **Photo ID** : A government-issued photo ID such as a driver’s license, passport, or state ID.
     * **Fingerprinting Request Form** : Some agencies may require an official request form, often provided by your employer or licensing authority.
     * **Payment Information** : Fees for fingerprinting and processing may need to be paid at the time of submission. It’s essential to know the amount and payment method ahead of time.
  2. **Choose a Fingerprinting Location** In Tennessee, fingerprinting can be done at a variety of authorized locations, including: 
     * **Tennessee Bureau of Investigation \(TBI\)** offices
     * **Live Scan Locations** : Many locations use live scan technology, where fingerprints are digitally captured, making the process faster and more accurate.
     * **Third-party Vendors** : Many private companies, like **Precisehire** , partner with TBI to provide fingerprinting services. These third-party services may offer added convenience, flexibility, and faster processing times.
  3. **Fingerprinting Session** During the fingerprinting session, your fingerprints will be taken either through **ink-based** or **live scan** methods. With **live scan** systems, the process involves placing your fingers on a digital scanner that records the prints and immediately sends them to the state or federal database. In contrast, ink-based methods involve manually rolling your fingers on a fingerprint card, which will then be submitted for processing. 
     * **Live Scan** : This is the preferred method in Tennessee due to its speed, efficiency, and accuracy.
     * **Ink Fingerprints** : While this method is less common, it is still accepted in some cases, especially for individuals outside the state or those requiring specific types of checks.
  4. **Submit to the Tennessee Bureau of Investigation \(TBI\)** After your fingerprints are collected, they are submitted to the **Tennessee Bureau of Investigation** for processing. The TBI will search the state’s criminal history database for any criminal records associated with your fingerprints. 
     * **State vs. National Checks** : A basic TN fingerprint background check will only search Tennessee’s records. However, if a national background check is required \(for example, for certain licensing or employment positions\), the fingerprints will also be submitted to the **Federal Bureau of Investigation \(FBI\)** for a broader search. This will check the national criminal databases for any relevant records.
     * **Criminal History Repository** : The TBI’s database includes arrests, convictions, and other relevant criminal history information within Tennessee. The FBI database includes national-level records, covering all 50 states and territories.
  5. **Wait for Processing and Results** The processing time for a TN fingerprint background check can vary. Typically, the results are available within a **few business days** to **a couple of weeks** , depending on the type of check requested and whether the check is being conducted solely at the state level or includes a national search. 
     * **Standard Checks** : These checks usually take 3-5 business days to complete.
     * **National Checks** : If FBI-level checks are required, the processing time may extend to 10-15 business days.
  6. **Receive Results** Once the fingerprint check is completed, the results are sent to the requesting agency, employer, or individual. The results will include a summary of the person’s criminal history, if any, based on the records found in the TBI or FBI databases. 
     * **Clear Report** : If no criminal records are found, the report will indicate that there are no disqualifying criminal convictions.
     * **Criminal Record Found** : If criminal records are found, the report will detail the offenses, dates, and outcomes, providing a comprehensive summary of the individual’s history.

It’s important to note that **not all criminal convictions are disqualifying**. Certain types of offenses may be considered minor or irrelevant to the position or license in question, depending on the industry’s requirements and specific job functions.

#### Legal Aspects of TN Fingerprint Background Checks

![Legal Aspects of TN Fingerprint Background Checks](https://precisehire.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Legal-Aspects-of-TN-Fingerprint-Background-Checks.png)

Fingerprint background checks in Tennessee, like those in many other states, are governed by specific laws and regulations. These rules help ensure that criminal history information is used fairly and responsibly, and they protect individuals’ rights to privacy. Below are some of the key legal aspects that affect TN fingerprint background checks:

##### 1\. **Tennessee State Laws and Regulations**

  * **Tennessee Bureau of Investigation \(TBI\) Regulations** : The TBI is the primary agency responsible for processing and maintaining criminal history information in Tennessee. When individuals undergo fingerprint background checks, the TBI must adhere to strict guidelines on what information can be released and how it can be used.
  * **State and Local Regulations** : Depending on the specific context \(e.g., employment, licensing\), other state or local regulations may apply. For instance, Tennessee has specific regulations for background checks in healthcare, education, and child care services.

##### 2\. **Use of Criminal History Information in Employment and Licensing**

  * **Fair Chance Act \(Ban the Box\)** : Tennessee, like many other states, has adopted policies like the **Ban the Box** law, which restricts employers from asking about criminal history on job applications. This law aims to give applicants with criminal records a fair chance at securing employment without being automatically disqualified based on their past.
  * **Disqualification Criteria** : Employers or licensing agencies must consider how the criminal history relates to the specific job or license. For example, certain convictions may disqualify someone from holding a healthcare position, while others might not be relevant for office-based jobs.

##### 3\. **Federal Regulations \(FCRA\)**

  * **Fair Credit Reporting Act \(FCRA\)** : The **FCRA** governs the use of background checks in the hiring process. It ensures that background checks are conducted fairly and that employers do not discriminate against applicants based on outdated or irrelevant criminal records.
  * **EEOC Guidelines** : The **Equal Employment Opportunity Commission \(EEOC\)** has set guidelines that govern how employers can use criminal history information. Employers must ensure that their background checks do not disproportionately impact certain groups, such as racial minorities, unless the criminal history directly affects the job.

##### 4\. **Privacy Concerns and Protection**

The **privacy of individuals** undergoing fingerprint background checks is a major concern. In Tennessee, several safeguards are in place to protect your personal data:

  * **Confidentiality** : Information obtained from a fingerprint background check is considered confidential. It can only be shared with authorized parties, such as employers or licensing agencies, who have a legitimate need for the information.
  * **Data Security** : Agencies like the TBI and other authorized vendors implement stringent security measures to protect the information you provide. This includes encryption, secure storage, and access restrictions to ensure that your personal data is not misused or accessed by unauthorized individuals.
  * **Consent** : Before undergoing a fingerprint background check, you will typically be asked to give written consent. This ensures that you understand the purpose of the check and agree to the process.

#### FAQs About TN Fingerprint Background Checks

Here are answers to some frequently asked questions about TN fingerprint background checks:

###  __How long does it take to get the results of a fingerprint background check in Tennessee?

The processing time for a **TN fingerprint background check** typically takes **3 to 10 business days** , depending on whether it’s a state-only check or includes a national FBI background check. Live scan fingerprinting is generally faster than ink-based methods.

###  __What is included in a fingerprint background check?

A **TN fingerprint background check** typically includes a search of the state’s criminal history database maintained by the **Tennessee Bureau of Investigation \(TBI\)**. It can reveal any arrests, convictions, or other criminal records. If a national check is requested, it will also include information from the **FBI** database.

###  __How long does a criminal record stay on file in Tennessee?

In Tennessee, criminal records are typically kept for **life**. However, certain offenses may be eligible for expungement, or the removal of a record, under specific circumstances. If an individual is eligible for expungement, they may petition the court to have their criminal record sealed or erased.

###  __What happens if I have a criminal record?

Having a **criminal record** doesn’t automatically disqualify you from employment or licensing. However, the nature of the conviction, how long ago it occurred, and how it relates to the position or license you’re seeking will all be considered. If you disagree with the information in your background check, you may challenge the results or provide documentation to explain the circumstances.

###  __Can I dispute incorrect information on my fingerprint background check?

Yes, if you believe there is an error on your **TN fingerprint background check** , you can file a dispute with the **Tennessee Bureau of Investigation \(TBI\)** or the FBI \(if a national check was conducted\). You will need to provide supporting documentation, such as court records, to prove that the information is incorrect.
