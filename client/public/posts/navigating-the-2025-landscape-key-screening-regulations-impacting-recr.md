### Understanding the 2025 Background Screening Regulations

The upcoming changes necessitate that HR professionals are well-informed and prepared to adapt. Compliance with these new screening regulations will not only ensure legal adherence but will also enhance the overall integrity of the recruitment process.

### Florida: New Background Screening for Healthcare Practitioners

In Florida, effective **July 1, 2025** , nearly all licensed healthcare professionals will be required to undergo a background screening when applying for an initial license or renewing their existing one. This regulation applies to practitioners governed by the Florida Department of Health’s Division of Medical Quality Assurance.

**Key Points of Florida ’s Regulation:**

  * **Mandatory Screening:** All licensed healthcare professionals will need to complete background checks, a significant change aimed at increasing the safety and trustworthiness of healthcare providers.
  * **Exemptions:** Certain professionals, including emergency medical technicians, paramedics, pharmacy interns, registered pharmacy technicians, and radiologic technologists \(unless applying through military active-duty spouse licensure pathways\) will be exempt from this requirement.
  * **Screening Process:** A Level 2 LiveScan fingerprinting process is mandatory, requiring practitioners to submit their documents to the state.
  * **Retention Requirements:** Fingerprints must be retained and renewed every five years, with strict timelines for compliance; noncompliance may result in license expiration or delays.

For more information, refer to [Florida’s New Background Screening Requirement](<https://fltraining.com/floridas-new-background-screening-requirement-july-01-2025/>) and [Florida Health Source](<https://flhealthsource.gov/background-screening/bgs-requirements/>).

### Illinois: Updated Employment Verification

In another significant move, **Public Act 103-0879** will take effect on **January 1, 2025** , in Illinois. This legislation updates the E-Verify system and Form I-9 compliance for employers in Illinois, fundamentally changing the employment verification landscape.

**Details on Illinois Compliance Requirements:**

  * **Widespread Application:** This requirement applies even to companies headquartered outside of Illinois, as long as they employ workers within the state.
  * **Enhanced Worker Authorization:** Employers will face tighter scrutiny in terms of worker authorization documentation, ensuring compliance with both federal and state laws.

Read more about this development in the compliance update from [ClearStar](<https://www.clearstar.net/february-2025-screening-compliance-update-2/>).

### National Trends: Transportation and Drug Testing

On a broader spectrum, there is a growing push at the federal level for enhanced background checks and drug testing in the transportation industry, especially for trucking. The American Trucking Associations has called for legislative support to increase drug testing and streamline background checks for truck drivers.

**Key Focus Areas Include:**

  * **Driver Safety:** Enhanced background checks aim to improve safety protocols and compliance, fostering a safer transportation environment for all.
  * **Broader Mandates:** Should the proposed legislation pass, it could lead to more stringent drug screening regulations across the sector.

More on this can be found through [ClearStar’s February 2025 Compliance Update](<https://www.clearstar.net/february-2025-screening-compliance-update-2/>).

### International Considerations: Spain’s Medical Marijuana Legalization

On an international level, the anticipated legalization of medical marijuana in Spain suggests forthcoming changes to screening standards for healthcare and other employment sectors. This evolution could lead to a reevaluation of how drug-testing policies are managed for companies engaging with Spanish residents.

For more information, visit [ClearStar’s Update](<https://www.clearstar.net/february-2025-screening-compliance-update-2/>).

### Other Employment Law Trends on the Radar for 2025

While these screening changes are crucial, employers in various sectors should also prepare for trends that, while not specific to screening itself, will impact compliance and operations. These include:

  * **Privacy Law Updates:** These may influence how criminal and credit checks are conducted.
  * **Overtime and Wage Law Changes:** New employee classifications and associated eligibility checks will be vital to monitor.
